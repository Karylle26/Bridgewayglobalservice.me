Our Purpose

Mission & Vision

We exist to make cross-border employment in Montenegro feel uncomplicated — for the company sending talent, and for the person arriving.

Mission

To be the most trusted concierge for visa, residency, and working-permit work in Montenegro — clearing the administrative path so businesses can focus on hiring the right people, and people can focus on arriving well.

Vision

A region where moving talent is as straightforward as moving capital. We want every employer who chooses Montenegro to feel they have a partner on the ground — calm, accountable, and ahead of every deadline.

Services

What we do

A complete administrative layer between you and the Montenegrin authorities — built for employers who hire continuously and need predictability.

01 / Documentation

Visa Documentation

Expert preparation and review of all immigration forms, translated and certified to local standards.

02 / Submission

Application Submission

Direct liaison with government authorities for filing — we walk every file through in person.

03 / Tracking

Status Monitoring

Real-time tracking and updates on application progress, with a single dashboard per employer.

04 / Residency

Boravak Requirements

Guidance on residency (Boravak) compliance, renewals, and dependent registrations.

05 / Compliance

Compliance Alerts

Proactive notifications for expirations, regulatory changes, and required actions — ahead of time.

06 / Support

Ongoing Support

Dedicated account management for all your expatriate staff, from arrival through long-term residency.
